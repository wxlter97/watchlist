- **Season 1:** a Loki variant escapes with the Tesseract in 2012 and the Time Variance Authority (TVA) catches him. He works with Mobius and meets Sylvie, another variant; they learn the TVA is run by He Who Remains, whom Sylvie kills. The timeline branches into a multiverse.
- **Season 2:** the TVA tries to contain the branches and the Temporal Loom fails. Loki learns to move through time at will and, to save every branch, takes the Loom's place: he sits at the end of time holding the multiverse together.
