- **Season 1:** Dracula launches his revenge on Wallachia and Trevor Belmont appears as a defender of the city.
- **Season 2:** Trevor, Sypha and Alucard join forces against Dracula's army.
- **Season 3:** Belmont and Sypha arrive in a town full of sinister secrets while Alucard mentors two admirers.
- **Season 4:** Belmont and Sypha study plans to resurrect Dracula while Alucard struggles to accept his humanity.
