- **Season 1:** Wilson Fisk is mayor of New York and creates a task force against vigilantes. Bullseye kills Foggy, Matt Murdock's best friend; Matt throws him off a building, breaking his no-killing rule, though Bullseye survives. Matt's girlfriend Heather kills Muse in self-defense. Matt returns as Daredevil to stop Fisk, who imposes martial law.
- **Season 2:** Bullseye tries to kill Fisk and his wife Vanessa dies. Fisk doubles down on hunting Daredevil. Karen Page goes on trial and Matt publicly reveals his identity in court, which frees her. Fisk erupts, Mr. Charles removes him from the city, Bullseye leaves with him, and Matt ends up in prison. Heather Glenn takes up the Muse mask.
