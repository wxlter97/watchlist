- Odin banishes Thor to Earth, powerless, for his arrogance; there he meets Jane Foster and Erik Selvig.
- Loki learns he was born a Frost Giant, son of Laufey, and takes Asgard's throne.
- Thor proves himself worthy, recovers Mjolnir and destroys the Bifrost to stop Loki; Loki falls into the void.
- Selvig ends up under S.H.I.E.L.D.'s watch, which is studying the Tesseract.
