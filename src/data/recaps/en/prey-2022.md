- 1719, the Great Plains: Naru, a young Comanche woman, wants to prove she's a hunter.
- A Predator lands on Earth and hunts whatever it sees as a threat: animals, French trappers and warriors.
- Her brother Taabe dies fighting it. Naru learns it can't see her if she lowers her body heat with a medicinal plant.
- She uses that edge and the Predator's own weapons to kill it, and comes home with its skull.
